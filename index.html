Before you ask

What this is — and isn't.

Is this a fitness app?

No. Fitness is one input. Meridian also reads sleep, recovery, nutrition, hormonal cycle and calendar load — then advises on mindset and nervous-system regulation, not just exercise.

What devices does it work with?

Any wearable or health platform with an API — Oura, Whoop, Apple Health, Garmin, lab providers and more. One profile, every source.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your data is yours. Nothing is shared, sold, or used to train models beyond your own coaching profile.

Why "by invitation"?

Each member is onboarded personally to calibrate their baseline. We're starting with a small cohort to get this exactly right before opening more broadly.
